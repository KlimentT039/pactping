# Privacy Policy for Witness

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

Witness ("the app", "we", "us") is an accountability app that lets you set a
personal goal with a deadline, name a witness from your phone's contacts, and
send that witness a text message if you miss the deadline. This policy explains
what data the app touches, how it is used, and — importantly — what it does
**not** do.

## The short version

Witness has **no account, no server, and no backend**. Everything the app does
happens on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your
personal data with anyone. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no
third-party trackers.

## Information the app accesses

**Contacts.** When you choose a witness, the app asks for permission to read
your contacts so you can pick a person and use their **name and phone number**.
That name and number are stored **only on your device** as part of the
commitment you create. No other contact information is read, and your contact
list is never uploaded anywhere.

**Goal, deadline, and confession text you enter.** The commitments you create —
including the goal description, the deadline, and the confession message you
write — are stored locally on your device so the app can remind you and, if you
miss a deadline, prepare the message to your witness.

**Photos (optional).** If you choose to attach a photo as proof when completing
a goal, that photo is used only within the app on your device.

**Notifications.** The app schedules local reminder notifications on your device
for your deadlines. These are generated on-device and are not sent through any
server.

## How messages to your witness are sent

When the app sends a heads-up or a confession to your witness, it does so by
handing a pre-filled message to **your phone's own SMS/messaging app**. The
message is sent from your phone, as you, using your normal carrier messaging.
Witness does not have its own messaging servers and never sends messages on its
own — you remain in control of what is sent.

## Data storage and retention

All data created in Witness is stored locally on your device. You can remove it
at any time by deleting a commitment or by uninstalling the app, which removes
the app's local data. Because there is no server, we hold no copy of your data.

## Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your personal information to third parties.
The only outbound communication the app facilitates is the SMS message you
explicitly choose to send to a witness you selected, delivered through your own
device's messaging app.

## Children's privacy

Witness is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Permissions summary

- **Read contacts** — to let you pick a witness and use their name and number.
- **Post notifications** — to remind you of your deadlines.
